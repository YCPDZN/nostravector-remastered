# NOSTRAVECTOR Remastered

*Exploration Division*

A story-driven 3D sci-fi exploration game that runs in the browser, on phones and desktops. It's set in **2125 AD**, thirty years after the Third World War, when humanity has only just reached the stars. Across five chapters you go from collapsing on the Antarctic ice to fighting for the whole Milky Way.

The full feature-by-feature history is in **[CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md)**.

---

## Play

1. Keep `index.html` and the `data/` folder together.
2. Open `index.html` in a modern browser. Double-clicking it works (file://), and no web server is needed.
3. Connect to the internet the first time, because Three.js and the fonts load from public CDNs. Everything else (models, music, voices) is in `data/`.

**itch.io:** zip `index.html` with the `data/` folder and upload the zip as an HTML game. `index.html` must be at the top level of the zip.

After him, back on Charon, Hak Chompjak calls you to Karribassa as Terra Imperia seizes Earth and Sol is carved up world by world, and you infiltrate the Cygnus Terra Imperius to face Hawthorne on its bridge. ## What you can do

- **Explore:** procedural Antarctica and Africa, real Earth landmarks seen from orbit, and a No Man's Sky-style galaxy with 500 systems, HD procedural planets, weather, moons and dwarf planets. You land anywhere, fly through atmospheres and warp between systems from the galaxy map.
- **Survive and build:** health, stamina, a jetpack, blocking and dodge rolls. Mine and chop for materials, craft tools, rifles and armour, smelt ores at the furnace, and build bases in five materials with snapping floors, walls, roofs and ramps. Teleporters link all your bases.
- **Fight:** wildlife, 495 native species and their lairs, lair bosses, Terra Imperia patrols, Pontifexes and Sisters of Hawthorne, and No Man's Sky-style dogfights in space. Beat faction leaders in their halls to win wars.
- **Farm Pontifexes and Sisters aboard the Cygnus:** after Hawthorne, fly back to her in orbit over Mercury and dock (or take the shuttle up from Mercury). Every officer and guard with a gold pip can be anointed when beaten, and whoever is hunting you waits on the bridge.
- **Lead a crew:** James, Orion, Nico, Khazglar, Charon and Kizna, Nyrexia, Croxian, Malia and more. Every companion takes five orders (**Mine · Explore · Base · Wander · Follow**), and their damage grows with their own level.
- **Rule:** Diplomacy with the Chompy Empire, the Erisian Empire, the Grek, the Human Federation of Sol, the Republic of Timpur and Terra Imperia. Five casus belli, colonies and governors, puppets, tributaries and resistance movements.
- **Choose:** KOTOR / Mass Effect-style alignment in every chapter, and choices that change who lives, who joins you and how Chapter IV ends.
- **Quests:** the main story, plus 134 hand-written side quests from traders and leaders.
- **Race (Chapters III–V):** the Archiostal Raceathon, across the landing pad from the Low-G Lounge. Talk to the race marshal and ride hoverboards round the Sanctum Ring Circuit: three laps against the regulars, any faction leader (or all of them at once), or your companions. Prizes, XP and records. `?race`, `?race=leaders` and `?race=comps` preview a race from the title.
- **Residents:** send a wanderer into the world and they live on as an AI "player" (up to 500). They mine, build bases, chat, trade, form guilds, run dungeons, found empires and wage wars, all on your device.
- **Puppets and blocs:** Timpur and Eris answer to the Chompy Empire (Eris to Terra Imperia while Alexia holds it), and in Chapter III the Grek answer to the Order and the Catharsis to the Grek. War on one brings in its whole bloc. You can also set two factions on each other: each calls its puppets, and one set on its own puppet loses it.
- **Enemy deputies:** every ruler you're at war with appoints a deputy, as you can: a resident picks a companion you offered them or their strongest guildmate, a faction leader a companion you lent them or one of their officers. Deputies defend worlds you bombard, counterattack and rally. Invade their world and beat them for the world and war score, then the ruler sends someone else to another world. On the main menu the leader stands behind their deputy, a hand on their shoulder, and every companion has lines for serving them.
- **Nostrapedia:** the in-game encyclopaedia of leaders, companions, ancient figures, Terra Imperia, the Primes and Vectors, and your own story's events.
- **Custom creations:** a species creator, a planet maker, ready-made and uploaded characters (.glb, rigged automatically), and an adventure maker you can play as anyone.

## Saves

- **The game never autosaves.** Choose **Save game** from the main menu or the pause menu. Unsaved changes are marked **UNSAVED**.
- **There are three slots.** Each holds all your wanderers and puts you back exactly where you saved, in every chapter: on foot, driving, in space, aboard the Nostra, or partway through an escape.
- **Saves are stored in the browser** (IndexedDB, with no practical size limit), so each browser and device keeps its own.
- **Export / Import** (Save / Load screen) moves everything to another browser or device in one `.json` file. Export now and then on itch.io, because browsers can wipe site storage.

## Repository layout

```
index.html        the whole game: code, UI and loader
data/             every packed asset (models, textures, music, voice packs), one <id>.js script each
assets/           source copies of the assets (.glb models, .mp3 audio, textures); the game doesn't load these
  audio/          music, voice lines by scene, e.g. nightmare_ch3/, james_ch3/, ending_ch4_bad/
  models/         characters, companions, leaders, primes, vectors, Terra Imperia, world props
  textures/       sky panoramas and terrain textures
nightmare/        Python build tools (rigging, model edits, duels) and their source models and music
CHANGELOG.md      the full history of every feature
```

### How assets work

- **Packed as scripts:** each asset in `data/` is a plain `<script>` that puts base64 data into an element with the same id (for example `nmErisVoices`). That's why the game runs from file:// with no server.
- **Loaded on demand:** big packs download only when needed (a cutscene, a companion, a leader's hall).
- **Voice packs** hold a JSON list of base64 MP3 clips. A scene refers to a clip by pack and slot (for example `['eris', 12]`). An empty or missing slot shows the line as subtitles instead.
- **Adding voice lines:** append the clip to its pack, point the line at the new slot, and keep the source MP3 in the matching `assets/audio/` folder.

### Preview links

Add one of these to the game's address to jump straight to a scene:

- `?residents=all` brings every resident to you.
- `?menu=ch4pre`, `?menu=ch4` or `?menu=ch5` previews the main menu for that chapter.
- `?ending=tared` plays the Taredesiban nightmare once the title screen is up.
- `?ending=neutralvillain` plays the neutral ending as a villain hears it (alignment −34 or lower).
- `?ending=goodvillain` plays the good ending as a villain lives it (the slaughter in the throne room, then your answer to Hak; his fight plays in the game).
- `?leader=hak&dep=orion` previews a faction leader's throne room with a deputy before them, the leader's hand on their shoulder (any `?leader=` key and any companion id; `dep=off0` to `off3` for one of the faction's own officers). `?villain&dep=croxian` does the same on the evil menu.
- `?villain` previews the evil main menu (a villain wanderer with the world in their hands; a hero at war with a villain resident's empire gets it too, with that resident on it); `?villain=chompy` (or `human`, `erisian`, `grek`, `taredesiban`, `oilkoumene`) starts it as that race. A switcher at the top (◀ ▶, or the [ and ] keys) cycles the villain through all six races, body and lines; leave the menu and your wanderer is themselves again, and nothing is saved; add `&menu=ch4` to see the blood red stacked on it.
- `?andromeda` loads a throwaway Chapter V wanderer (never saved) in space at Sagittarius A*: press Continue, choose a galaxy (G or the button: Andromeda, the Large or the Small Magellanic Cloud), then fly into the black hole to cross.
- `?hakchompjakbossfightevilgoodending` loads a throwaway villain wanderer (never saved) on Carax and takes them straight to Hak's fight at the top of the Carax Bastion. Press Continue on the title screen.
- `?finalboss=good`, `?finalboss=neutral`, `?finalboss=neutraloriondead` or `?finalboss=bad` plays the arrival at Sagittarius A*: Zark's first-person flight out of the black hole (with the Catharsis Orion beside him for `neutraloriondead`), Orion's call on `bad`, then the crew's scene for that road. Tap once the title screen is up; nothing is saved.
- `?ending=oilkoumene` plays the Oilkoumene nightmare (The Fall of Obra-Spira), and `?ending=oilkdream` plays the Oilkoumene dream on the ice (Tyrotaronus). Tap once the title screen is up; nothing is saved.
- `?badchoices` loads a test wanderer in Chapter IV with every choice made the wrong way: Nyrexia never recruited, James, Croxian and Malia killed, and Orion gone to Hawthorne alive. So Orion's boss fight waits on Earth, and the final battle plays the bad ending. It starts after the two years, aboard the Jewel of Archamus in Sol, at level 40 or higher. It uses your active wanderer's look, press Continue to play, and it can't be saved.
- `?ch5allteam=evil` (or `=good`, `=neutral`) loads a test wanderer in Chapter V with every companion in the crew (plus a Pontifex and a Sister), all pushed fully that way, aboard the Nostra. It uses your active wanderer's look, press Continue to play, and it can't be saved.
