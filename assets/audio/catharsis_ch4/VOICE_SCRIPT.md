# The Catharsis speak · the outpost on Tallax IV (Chapter IV)

When you take the carapace rifle, the screen darkens and the Catharsis speak ten lines. Each line is recorded twice: in the Catharsis' own language, and in English. **Settings → Catharsis speech** (also in the pause menu's Options, and as a button on the dark screen) chooses which you hear. In Catharsis, the subtitles show their words with the English in brackets.
Packs: `data/cathOutpostVoices.js` (CH4CATHARSIS1–10) and `data/cathOutpostEnVoices.js` (CH4CATHARSISEN1–10). **All 20 recorded.**

## The Jewel retaken: the Catharsis speak in your language

When you clear the bridge, the screen darkens and the Catharsis speak ten lines, in English (they've chosen to), in their demonic voice. No language switch here.
Pack: `data/cathWinVoices.js` (CH4CATHARSISWIN1–10). **All 10 recorded.**

| # | File | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | CH4CATHARSISWIN1.mp3 | You are celebrating already. We can hear it in your breathing, in the sudden confidence of the creatures standing beside you. A few bodies fall across a metal floor, and you mistake survival for victory. |
| 2 | CH4CATHARSISWIN2.mp3 | You understand us now because we have chosen to use your language. There will be no refuge in mistranslation. Every word that follows is meant for you, Wanderer, and we intend for you to remember it. |
| 3 | CH4CATHARSISWIN3.mp3 | Those bodies were an advance brood. They entered your corridors to learn how you move, where you retreat, and which of your companions will step into danger for you. You fought for your lives. We watched and learned. |
