# The Catharsis speak · the outpost on Tallax IV (Chapter IV)

When you take the carapace rifle, the screen darkens and the Catharsis speak ten lines. Each line is recorded twice: in the Catharsis' own language, and in English. **Settings → Catharsis speech** (also in the pause menu's Options, and as a button on the dark screen) chooses which you hear. In Catharsis, the subtitles show their words with the English in brackets.
Packs: `data/cathOutpostVoices.js` (CH4CATHARSIS1–10) and `data/cathOutpostEnVoices.js` (CH4CATHARSISEN1–10). **All 20 recorded.**
