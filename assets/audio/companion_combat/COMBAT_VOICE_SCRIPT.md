# Companion combat lines

Each companion has **five combat lines while they're Hero or Neutral**, and **five once they're an Awakened Villain** (their own alignment chart at −34 or below). One line per moment, in this order:

1. **Fight starts:** the first threat of a fight
2. **Kill:** they drop an enemy
3. **Hurt:** something hostile gets right up against them
4. **You're in trouble:** your health falls under 30%
5. **Victory:** the fight's over, if anything died

Packs: `data/cmb<Name>Voices.js` (COMBAT<NAME>1–5) and `data/cmb<Name>EvilVoices.js` (COMBAT<NAME>EVIL1–5). A missing pack shows its lines as subtitles. Only companions travelling with you speak, one at a time; each line has a cool-down so they don't repeat themselves.
