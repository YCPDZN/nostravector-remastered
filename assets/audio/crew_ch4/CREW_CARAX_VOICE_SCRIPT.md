# Your crew before Carax (Chapter IV, the path of no return)

After Zark's flight (and Orion's call, on the road to the bad ending), aboard the Jewel with Carax ahead. Every story companion in your party says one line; imported characters, Pontifexes and uplifted crew don't speak, and companions who died (or were never recruited, or aren't yours) have no line on that road. The mood follows your road: **good** hopeful, **neutral** grim, **bad** already beaten.

Voiced (recorded as CH4GOOD1–15, CH4NEUTRAL1–15 and CH4BAD1–10, kept here as CH4CREWGOOD/NEUTRAL/BAD so they don't clash with the ending's own CH4GOOD/CH4BAD clips). One pack per mood, a clip per line in this order: `data/crewCaraxGood.js`, `data/crewCaraxNeutral.js`, `data/crewCaraxBad.js`.
