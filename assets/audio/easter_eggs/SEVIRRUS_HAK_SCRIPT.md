# Easter egg · Lord Sevirrus and Hak Chompjak V (Chapters III–V)

Bring **Lord Sevirrus** (from *Sevirrus*) along in your party and speak to **Hak Chompjak V**: on Aridaxx in Chapter III once you've met him, or as a leader in Chapter V. Hak *was* Sevirrus, three hundred years ago.
**Sevirrus:** young, arrogant, delighted with himself, cruel; he snaps his neck to one side mid-stare. **Hak:** old, quiet, tired and steady; he never raises his voice, and the last line is the closest he comes to breaking.
