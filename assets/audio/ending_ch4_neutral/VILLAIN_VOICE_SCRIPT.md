# The neutral ending · the villain's version (Chapter IV)

If you reach the neutral ending with your alignment at −34 or lower (the villain's road), the same duel plays (Zark rises, Archamus comes down and drives him off through a rift), with these words instead. The hero's and neutral alignment keep the original. Preview: `?ending=neutralvillain`.
Record as CH4NEUTRALVILLAIN1–8; the game picks them up from `data/c4eNeutralVillainVoices.js`.

| CH4NEUTRALVILLAIN3 | Lord Zark | Archamus. You came down from your light to save me from your own champion? How the stars have turned. |
