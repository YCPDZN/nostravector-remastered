# Hak Chompjak, Prince of Zark · voice script

The second phase of Hak's fight in the villain's good ending (Chapter IV). Beat Hak once and he doesn't fall: on his knees among the dead, he calls on Lord Zark's dark and Archamus's light, and becomes Hak Chompjak, Prince of Zark, once more. Preview: `?hakprinceofzark` (the cutscene, then the fight), or `?hakchompjakbossfightevilgoodending` (both phases).

**Voices:** every line of his is Hak's own voice. Before the change he is broken and venomous, nearly a whisper, until line 4, where he roars. Once he's the Prince he is the most sinister thing in the game: low, slow, savouring every word, smiling through the cruelty. He almost never shouts; when he does (the crown cracking, the enrage) it should feel like something tearing loose. Lord Zark and Archamus appear as ghosts but say nothing: every line is Hak's.

**Pack:** `data/hakPrinceVoices.js` (HAKPRINCEOFZARK1–26, in this order: slot = number − 1). **All 26 lines recorded** (the source MP3s are in this folder; recorded as CH4HAKFINALBOSS1–26).
