# Out of the black hole: Zark, Hawthorne and Orion (Chapter IV, the path of no return)

A first-person cutscene through Zark's eyes, on the first warp into Sagittarius A* on the path of no return. Zark, the Catharsis Hawthorne and (if you shot Orion down in orbit) the Catharsis Orion tear through the galaxy's fleets on the way to Carax, gloating about ending all life.

Voiced: CH4LORDZARK1–18 (in this folder), every line in speaking order, packed as `data/zarkPovVoices.js`. Orion's four (5, 9, 13, 16) are skipped when he isn't there.
